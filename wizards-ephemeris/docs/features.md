# Features

Seventeen bodies:

Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, the true node, mean Lilith, Chiron, Ceres, Pallas, Juno, and Vesta.

Each position carries ecliptic longitude, latitude, and longitude speed. Equatorial right ascension and declination are available for parallel and contra-parallel aspects.

Ten house systems: Placidus, Whole Sign, Koch, Equal, Regiomontanus, Campanus, Porphyry, Alcabitius, Sripati, and Polich-Page. The result includes the ascendant, the midheaven, ARMC, and the vertex. Near the poles, Placidus and Koch fall back to Porphyry.

Zodiacs: tropical, and sidereal. Six ayanamsas: Lahiri, Raman, Fagan-Bradley, Krishnamurti, True Citra, and Galactic center (Mula). Sidereal nutation is true or mean. Whole-sign cusps can snap after the ayanamsa shift, or snap the tropical sign first. Mean Lilith is either the inclined apogee or the ecliptic longitude. The combinations, and which tradition uses which, are in [traditions.md](traditions.md).

Time: Julian day, the proleptic Gregorian calendar, and delta T.
